Week 4 · SportsCenter

Adams and Jefferson hang 190 on the league

Four weeks in, the league has a clear top three. Ben E., michael and CeeDeez Nutz are the three highest scorers, all averaging 138 or better, and a combined 106-26 when you line them up against every team every week. Behind them, the schedule is doing a lot of the talking. Team Galakrond has scored enough to be 2-2 and is 0-4 after facing 623 points, by far the most in the league. Bingo Night has scored like a half-win team and is 2-2. And Lyudmil just posted 190.06, the best single week anybody has had this season.

Record carries the most weight now, with recent form, scoring and luck behind it. Where a blurb mentions expected wins, that is a team's record against the whole league each week, scaled to four games: what its scoring has earned, regardless of schedule. The color bar splits this week's points by position.

Ben's Brilliant Team

Ben Elton
133.4 4-0 · +2.0 vs proj

The only unbeaten team, and the record holds up. Ben E. is 37-7 against the whole league over four weeks, scoring between 131 and 151 every week. The engine is the receiver room, about 22 points a week above league average, with Jaxon Smith-Njigba at 116.7 on the season. The backfield is the thin spot, about four a week below average. Expected wins are 3.36, so there is a little luck in the 4-0, most visibly this week's 0.2 point escape against CeeDeez Nutz. Still a legitimate first-place team.

Adams and Jefferson▲3

The power score has noticed all of it and moves this team up three spots.
