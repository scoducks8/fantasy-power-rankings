Oregon owns the Horseshoe.

Oregon 41, Ohio State 28. The fantasy rankings are below. They are less important.

Eugene, Oregon. Track Town USA. The town where Bill Bowerman poured rubber into his wife's waffle iron, where Phil Knight ran for him, and where it never rains inside Autzen Stadium. On Saturday, November 7th, the Ducks walked into Ohio Stadium, fell behind 14-0, and scored 38 straight. Dylan Raiola threw for 257 yards and two touchdowns. Dierre Hill Jr. ran for two more, one from 48 yards out. They converted a fake punt on fourth-and-9 in the Horseshoe because they could. Oregon 41, Ohio State 28. Greatest program in the history of organized sport. Here are your fantasy rankings.
