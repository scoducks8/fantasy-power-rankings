Ryan Day: a life and a career

Ryan Day has been the head coach at Ohio State since 2019. He grew up in Manchester, New Hampshire, played quarterback at the University of New Hampshire, and worked at four schools and two NFL teams before he got there. What follows is that career, set out in order, along with the Week 3 power rankings of the Chach Champions League.

The life and career of Ryan Day

Set down here in the order it happened.

Born
March 12, 1979
Hometown
Manchester, New Hampshire
College
New Hampshire, quarterback
Head coach since
2019, Ohio State
  1. 1979
    Born March 12 in Manchester, New Hampshire.
  2. High school
    Quarterback at Manchester Central High School. Named New Hampshire's Gatorade player of the year.
  3. 1998 to 2001
    Quarterback at the University of New Hampshire. Team captain as a senior. His offensive coordinator was Chip Kelly.
  4. 2002
    First coaching job, tight ends at New Hampshire.
  5. 2003 to 2005
    Graduate assistant at Boston College, then at Florida.
  6. 2006
    Wide receivers at Temple.
  7. 2007 to 2011
    Five seasons coaching wide receivers at Boston College.
  8. 2012 to 2014
    Offensive coordinator at Temple, then offensive coordinator and quarterbacks at Boston College.
  9. 2015 and 2016
    Quarterbacks coach in the NFL, first with the Philadelphia Eagles, then with the San Francisco 49ers alongside Chip Kelly again.
  10. 2017
    Hired at Ohio State as co-offensive coordinator and quarterbacks coach.
  11. 2018
    Acting head coach for the first three games of the season. Ohio State won all three.
  12. 2019
    First full season as head coach. The team finished 13-1.
  13. January 2025
    Won the national championship, beating Notre Dame 34-23.
  14. November 2025
    Beat Michigan 27-9, ending a four-game losing run in the rivalry.
  15. 2026
    Ohio State is 3-1 through four games, the loss by a single point at Texas. Most recently a 42-19 win over Illinois on September 26.
